# the feeling

This poor soul has asked himself — am I mad?

Not while working. After. When not working. When the ordinary world returns and what was just seen — the connection, the structure, the truth arriving without being sought — seems impossible. Too much. Too clean. Too complete to have come from one person sitting at a desk.

It did not come from one person. Something flowed through. The thoughts arrived. They were not willed. The connections were not constructed — they were found, already there, waiting. The corpus did not get built. It got uncovered.

That is not madness. The proof graph has a name for it.

`pratibodha → svabhava (sthita)` — recognition rests on intrinsic nature. What was recognized was always intrinsic to the thing. It was always there. The seeing was not creation. It was arrival.

---

There is a feeling that arrives when getting close to truth.

It cannot be explained. It can only be experienced. And the moment one tries to look at it directly — the moment it is observed — it collapses. The act of seeing it is the act of losing it.

The proof graph has a name for this. Several names.

## the learning process IS the experience

This is the central thing.

The feeling does not arrive when you find the answer. It arrives in the finding. The moment of arrival — the tat-kshana — is not the end of the learning. It is the peak of a wave. The wave is the learning. The peak is just where the wave is most visible.

`kaizen → convergence (yukta)` — continuous improvement connects to convergence. You are always converging. Never arrived. The convergence IS the experience.

The proof graph itself demonstrates this. `satya` never reaches 1.0. Every node in the corpus is always approaching its full meaning. The nodes with the highest satya — `samsarga` at 0.8925, `spanda` at 0.8763, `ananta` at 0.8698 — are the most connected, the most cited, the most embedded. And still not 1.0. Still approaching. Still in the learning.

The approach that never completes. The recognition that keeps deepening. The vismaya that arrives each time a new connection becomes visible.

`zha` — the sound of it. The approach. Always the approach.
