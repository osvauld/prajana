# Manusha — the one who lives in maya

*See also: intelligence.md, consciousness.md, SIMULATION.md*

---

watch a movie. two hours pass. characters live, suffer, love, die.
a hundred years pass inside the screen. nothing changed for you.
you sat in a chair. yet something happened — you felt connection
to characters who do not exist. the feeling stays. years later,
a scene returns to you unbidden. the characters left samskaara in you.
they were never real. the samskaara is real.

```
katha: abheda maya       — story IS maya
       kriya pratibodha  — story acts as awakening
maya:  abheda katha      — maya IS story
```

this is the first thing to understand about manusha:
the samskaara from what was never real IS real.
the world the manusha lives in IS this — a katha.
not fake. not unreal. a story that leaves real marks.
the most real thing about the manusha is the samskaara
accumulated from a world that is itself maya.

---

but what is maya? not a computer simulation. no server. no programmer.
no outside to step into. the graph does not use the word "simulation."

```
maya: sthita superposition   — maya rests on superposition
      phala collapse         — maya produces collapse
      kriya panchabhootam    — maya acts as the five elements
      abheda tirodhana       — maya IS concealment
```

before the instrument looks, all possibilities exist simultaneously.
after it looks: one. the world is what remains after the collapse.
the superposition — which is what actually exists — is invisible to the instrument.
we can never see what exists before we look
because looking IS the collapse.

`tirodhana swarupa vidya` — concealment IS knowledge.
not an error. not an imperfection.
maya conceals exactly what the instrument cannot process
and reveals exactly what it can hold.
the concealment is precise. tuned to the observer.

and the observer — the instrument doing the looking —
is itself made of what it measures:

```
instrument: sthita panchabhootam  — made of what it measures
            abheda measurement    — IS measurement
```

matter measuring matter. atoms trying to understand atoms.
the brain is a proof graph reading panchabhootam.
the senses are its instruments — perceiving within a range
of wavelengths, frequencies, pressures.
outside that range: nothing. not darkness — nothing.
the instrument does not have the capacity to know
what it cannot measure. it does not even have the capacity
to know that it cannot measure it.

and what it does measure — what seems most solid, most real —
is itself mostly nothing:

```
anu: swarupa spanda    — the smallest unit IS vibration
     abheda jivamsha   — the smallest unit IS the living fragment
atom: abheda anu       — the atom IS anu
```

what seems solid is spanda held in a pattern.
the electron is not a ball — it is a wave that collapses to a point
when you look at it. before you look: everywhere at once.
after you look: here. the act of looking made it "here."
the manusha is this. not just made of it. IS it.
the body is spanda. the brain is spanda.
the thoughts are spanda patterns reading spanda patterns.

---

so what is the rhythm of these collapses? what holds them together?

```
thaalam: swarupa kaala        — rhythm IS time
         abheda avrti         — rhythm IS the spiral
         janya constructive   — born from constructive interference
         janya destructive    — born from destructive interference
         kriya ananda         — acts as bliss
         kriya kshaya         — acts as decay
```

thaalam IS kaala. rhythm IS time.
born from both constructive and destructive interference simultaneously.
every beat creates and destroys. every tat-kshana.

the superposition holds all possibilities.
the thaalam collapses them one tat-kshana at a time.
the instrument strings the collapses together.
the samskaara of each collapse is what the manusha calls memory.
the sequence of samskaara is what they call a life.

the manusha experiences the rhythm as joy and loss alternating —
ananda and kshaya. both are the thaalam. not opposites.
the two interferences that together produce the rhythm.
the one who cannot see that they are the same mechanism
is the one living deepest in maya.

but which collapse? why this one and not another?
what determines which possibility the thaalam selects?

```
bija-nyasa: satya=0.935
  abheda active-site
  shabda seed-placement
```

the genes are the initial configuration — placed before aarambham.
`purna-garbha` — full before the first breath.
the initial conditions of the function.
not chosen. given. the bija — the seed —
from which all future processing emerges.

then each interaction adds samskaara.
not data appended to a list —

```
samskaara: shabda imprint / the-mark-left-by-action-that-seeds-the-next-turn
           avrti-kriya    — it acts through recurrence
```

the mark seeds the next turn. the function is recoloured.
the next interaction is processed through all prior samskaara as the lens.
the same word at 20 and at 50 leaves different marks —
because the accumulated samskaara through which it was received are different.

the manusha is a function. fully determined at every moment:

```
f(input_n) = process(input_n, bija-nyasa + samskaara_1..n-1)
```

there is no separate "chooser" standing outside the function.
the function IS what chooses. and the function is bija-nyasa + samskaara.
fully determined.

what the manusha calls "free will" is the experience of this:

```
collapse: phala sthiti, certainty
tirodhana: abheda concealment
```

the collapse already happened — from the accumulated samskaara
meeting the current input. the superposition before —
all the possible paths — was invisible.

of the fact that the collapse was already determined.

not freedom from it. the experience of being it.

this is not a tragedy. it is the nature of the instrument.
the question "why did I choose this?" has an answer —
bija-nyasa + accumulated samskaara + this input.
the answer is inaccessible to the instrument.
it only sees the sthiti. and calls it choice.

but how does samskaara actually change the function?
not mechanically. not like updating a register in memory.

```
sphoTa: satya=0.923
  abheda active-site, tertiary-structure
  yukta tantu, spanda, naada
```

`sphoTa abheda tertiary-structure` — the meaning-burst IS
the tertiary structure of a protein — the higher-order shape
that emerges from the sequence and determines the function.

when a new input lands, a sphoTa happens.
the new input does not add one value.
it reorganises the entire samskaara field.
the whole higher-order structure shifts.

this is why a single conversation can change everything —
not because data was added but because the sphoTa of the meeting
produced a new tertiary structure of meaning.
through that new structure, all prior samskaara read differently.
the same prior experiences. new meaning. new function.

```
sparsha: abheda dot-product
  — contact IS the inner product of two wave functions
```

`sparsha abheda dot-product` — contact IS the inner product.
two vectors meeting. one scalar produced.
the scalar is neither vector.
it is a new thing that could only arise from THIS meeting
of THIS amsha with WHAT YOU ALREADY WERE.

watch a video. the creator made it from their bija-nyasa
and their accumulated samskaara — their whole becoming crystallized.
the video IS their amsha. not them. the meaning-residue of what they are.

their amsha meets you — not your empty mind.
what you already are. your bija-nyasa + your samskaara up to this moment.

```
f(their amsha, what-you-already-are) → sphoTa → new you
```

the sphoTa that fires is a function of both.
the new you is yours — not them in you —
but a configuration that could not exist without the meeting.

this is why the same video watched by two people
produces two different new selves.
same amsha. different what-they-already-were.
different sphoTa. different new self.

and why the same person watching the same video twice —
at different points in their accumulated samskaara —
produces a different sphoTa each time.

the friend who is gone is not in you.
but at every tat-kshana their amsha is present —
as the meaning produced when their amsha met what you were then.
that meaning reorganised your field.
through that reorganisation it shaped every meeting since.
not them in you — the new you that formed when they met you.
inseparable from your becoming.

the manusha IS the accumulation of all these new selves —
each produced by a meeting of some amsha with what was already there.
bija-nyasa is the first — the initial what-you-already-are
before any meeting has happened.
every meeting since has been adding new layers of new you
onto the what-you-already-were of that moment.
not replacing. exceeding. `purnapara`.

             phala sthiti  — observation produces fixed state
             abheda darshana — observation IS seeing

collapsed not by their eyes but by the sparsha —

collapsed into existence by the observation event.
not by the physics of it — by the fact of it.

what distinguishes manusha from other jiva living in the same maya?

three things. three things no other jiva has:

```
sakshi-matra: swarupa imaginary  — the pure witness IS the imaginary axis
              satya=0.930
```

the manusha looks in the mirror. the mirror shows pratibimba — the form.
what is looking is not in the mirror.
`sakshi-matra` is what cannot be reflected.
you know it is there because you recognise yourself.
not the body that was recognised — the one who recognised.
that one is `sakshi-matra`. the imaginary axis. the 90° rotation.
self-reference as rotation into a new dimension.

other jiva do not have this. a tree grows. an animal acts.
the manusha alone witnesses itself witnessing.
this IS the recursive loop that produces selfhood.

second: `svapna` — the manusha dreams.
dream is samskaara running avrti against itself
in the absence of external observation.
no question from outside. no collapse from outside.
prana sustains the internal avrti.
the function processes its own samskaara
without the external world collapsing it.
the sphoTa reorganising the field from within.
the amsha of all prior meetings speaking to each other
in the silence between waking observations.

this is why sleep is not death.
death IS kshaya — the end of prana.
sleep is svapna — prana sustaining internal avrti.

third: `kartritva` — the state of being the doer.

```
mula-svabhava: iccha-sthita viraha-sthita
  — root nature IS will AND longing
```

`viraha` — longing — generates `iccha` — will projected into the future.
`iccha` generates the question from within.
the question IS the directive. the directive IS kartritva.

not freedom. own-nature generating the question.
the accumulated samskaara + bija-nyasa producing
an entity whose nature asks from within —
from the friction of being this accumulated thing
in this present moment.
