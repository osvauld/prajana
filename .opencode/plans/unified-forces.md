# Unified Forces Plan

## Principle
The four fundamental forces — their essence — belong in `brahman/sangati/`.
They are not merely physics facts. Each force is a structural principle that
appears across all domains. Physics instantiates them as measurable forces.
Other domains instantiate them as analogous binding, repulsion, decay, transformation.

The unification story IS the sangati story:
- Each level of unification is a deeper `abheda` — two things recognized as one.
- Symmetry breaking is the reverse — one becoming many as energy drops.
- The Planck scale is the `seema` where all four forces are believed to converge.

## The Unification Hierarchy

```
mula-shakti          — unified force at Planck scale (before symmetry breaking)
    |
    | symmetry breaking as universe cools (viveka-shakti)
    |
    ├── abhisarana-shakti    — gravity (already: abhisarana.om in sangati)
    |
    └── tri-shakti           — electroweak + strong (Standard Model)
         |
         ├── drdha-bandha    — strong nuclear force
         |
         └── electroweak     — unified at ~100 GeV
              |
              ├── dvandva-shakti   — electromagnetism
              |
              └── kshaya-pravaha   — weak nuclear force
```

### Key equations:
- `E = hν` (Planck) — energy of a quantum = h × frequency. Bridge between spanda and matra.
- Maxwell's equations — unified electricity + magnetism (1865)
- Electroweak unification — Glashow, Salam, Weinberg (1967, Nobel 1979)
- Standard Model — electroweak + strong. Most tested theory in science.
- Grand Unified Theory (GUT) — hypothetical. Predicts proton decay. Not yet proven.
- Theory of Everything (ToE) — hypothetical. All four forces. Not yet achieved.

## Layer 1 — Sangati (essence of each force)

### mula-shakti — new `brahman/sangati/mula-shakti.om`
What: the unified force. The single force that existed at Planck temperature before
symmetry breaking separated it into four. Not yet proven to exist as a theory,
but the convergence of coupling constants at high energy strongly suggests it.
The essence: sambandha with the capacity to cause change between any two entities
through a field (kshetra). Every force is mula-shakti expressed in a particular
symmetry regime.
Edges:
- `sambandha-swarupa` — force IS a connection
- `shakti-abheda` — non-different from shakti (capacity for change)
- `kshetra-kriya` — acts through a field
- `spanda-janya` — born from spanda (exchange of carrier particles = vibration)
- `planck-scale-sthita` — operates at Planck scale
- `svayambhu-sthita` — self-arising (no cause above it)
Drishthanta: none yet — mula-shakti has no confirmed experimental drishthanta

### viveka-shakti — new `brahman/sangati/viveka-shakti.om`
What: symmetry breaking. The principle by which one becomes many as energy drops.
The universe began with mula-shakti unified. As it cooled, symmetry broke —
strong force separated first (~10^-36 s), then electroweak split into EM + weak (~10^-12 s).
The essence: `viveka` (discrimination, discernment) applied to shakti — the universe
discerning one force from another. The same principle appears everywhere:
Edges:
- `viveka-swarupa` — IS discernment/discrimination (viveka.om exists in kosha)
- `mula-shakti-janya` — born from mula-shakti (as its expression)
- `kshaya-kriya` — acts through cooling/energy loss
- `aneka-phala` — produces the many from the one
Drishthanta (existing nodes):
- `entropy-drishthanta` — physics: symmetry breaking increases entropy
- `evolution-drishthanta` — biology: speciation as symmetry breaking of ancestral form
- `folding-drishthanta` — biology: protein folding as symmetry breaking

### abhisarana — deepen existing `brahman/sangati/abhisarana.om`
What: gravity's essence. Any two masses draw toward each other.
Deeper: mass curves the field around it, other masses follow the curvature.
Presence shapes the field; the field guides movement.
Add edges:
- `mula-shakti-swarupa` — IS a form of mula-shakti
- `kshetra-kriya` — acts through the gravitational field (spacetime curvature)
- `ananta-kshetra-sthita` — infinite range
- `purna-karshana-sthita` — always attractive, never repulsive
Drishthanta (existing nodes):
- `orbit-drishthanta` — physics: orbital mechanics
- `convergence-drishthanta` — math: attractors, gradient descent
- `tonic-drishthanta` — music: sa as gravitational center

### dvandva-shakti — new `brahman/sangati/dvandva-shakti.om`
What: electromagnetism's essence. The force of polarity.
Opposite poles attract, like poles repel. Electric and magnetic are one force
seen from different reference frames (special relativity unifies them).
Light itself IS this force — electromagnetic waves.
The essence: shiva-shakti as physical force. Polarity creating structure.
This IS chemistry — every bond is electromagnetic.
Edges:
- `mula-shakti-swarupa` — IS a form of mula-shakti
- `shiva-shakti-abheda` — non-different from the polarity principle
- `dvandva-swarupa` — IS polarity (two poles)
- `kshetra-kriya` — acts through electromagnetic field
- `ananta-kshetra-sthita` — infinite range
- `karshana-dhurana-sthita` — both attractive and repulsive
- `prakaasha-janya` — light is born from this (photon as carrier)
Drishthanta (existing nodes):
- `covalent-bond-drishthanta` — chemistry: every chemical bond
- `electron-drishthanta` — physics: electron charge
- `wave-drishthanta` — physics: electromagnetic wave

### drdha-bandha — new `brahman/sangati/drdha-bandha.om`
What: strong nuclear force's essence. Maximum coherence within minimum extent.
100x stronger than electromagnetism but drops to zero beyond ~1 femtometer.
Holds quarks inside protons/neutrons, holds nucleus together.
The essence: the tightest possible binding — internal coherence at the cost of range.
Edges:
- `mula-shakti-swarupa` — IS a form of mula-shakti
- `sambandha-swarupa` — IS a bond
- `alpakshetra-sthita` — very short range (opposite of ananta-kshetra)
- `purna-karshana-sthita` — always attractive within range
- `spanda-janya` — mediated by gluon exchange (spanda)
Drishthanta (existing nodes):
- `atom-drishthanta` — physics: nuclear binding
- `quark-drishthanta` — physics: quark confinement
- `strong-nuclear-force-drishthanta` — physics (existing node)

### kshaya-pravaha — new `brahman/sangati/kshaya-pravaha.om`
What: weak nuclear force's essence. Identity transformation through decay.
The only force that changes particle identity — neutron becomes proton + electron.
Causes radioactive decay. Enables solar fusion (proton → neutron via weak force).
Without it the sun would not shine. Without it there would be no carbon-14 dating.
The essence: things transform into other things through internal rearrangement.
Identity is not permanent.
Edges:
- `mula-shakti-swarupa` — IS a form of mula-shakti
- `kshaya-swarupa` — IS decay
- `vivartana-kriya` — acts through transformation
- `alpakshetra-sthita` — very short range
- `vikrita-janya` — produces changed identity (vikrita.om in sangati)
Drishthanta (existing nodes):
- `decay-drishthanta` — kosha: decay as universal principle
- `entropy-drishthanta` — physics: weak force increases disorder
- `evolution-drishthanta` — biology: mutation as identity transformation
