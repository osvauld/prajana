Kosha is the talker — 2135 edges to sangati, 296 to bhasha, 196 to mantra. Sangati is almost silent outward — only 80 edges to kosha, 4 to bhasha, 0 to mantra. Sangati speaks to itself (1489) and points nowhere (130).

The asymmetry: kosha→sangati has 2135 edges. sangati→kosha has 80. Domain knowledge depends on structural truth, but structural truth barely acknowledges domain knowledge. This is philosophically correct — sangati IS the ground, kosha grows from it.
